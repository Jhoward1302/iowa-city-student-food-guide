THE HERKY EXPERIENCE

Loyalty That Makes Sense

A restaurant loyalty program should reward repeat visits without making customers memorize complicated rules. Our fictional Herky Rewards concept uses a straightforward points system so guests can understand the value of returning.

We imagine members earning one point for every dollar spent on eligible purchases, with sample rewards available at different point levels. These numbers are illustrative, not actual offers, and the restaurant does not have a live membership program.

A real loyalty system would need secure accounts, clear eligibility rules, expiration policies, privacy disclosures, and safeguards against fraudulent activity. Our demonstration intentionally avoids collecting email addresses, phone numbers, or purchase histories.

THE HERKY EXPERIENCE

Making Every Visit Count

Rewards are most useful when they support an already enjoyable restaurant experience. Guests should return because they like the food and hospitality, not because a promotion makes them feel pressured to spend.

The interactive calculator below illustrates how points could accumulate. It does not create an account, issue a reward, or track a customer. Gift cards are a separate fictional feature for guests who want to share a meal with someone else.

THE HERKY EXPERIENCE

What Herky Stands For

Herky Steakhouse is an imagined Iowa City restaurant built around a simple idea: a great dinner should feel special without feeling exclusive. Our concept blends generous portions, warm service, familiar favorites, and a comfortable room where families, friends, students, and coworkers can gather. Every offering on this site is a fictional example created for a digital marketing course.

The proposed downtown location is part of the story rather than an operating address. The building visualization shows a possible street-level restaurant with apartments above, not an existing business or an approved construction project. The hours, prices, menu descriptions, job opportunities, and promotions are illustrative and should not be treated as available services.

We designed the experience for different kinds of evenings: a quick lunch between commitments, a family meal after a long week, a birthday celebration, or a relaxed date night. The menu aims for approachable choices alongside premium cuts, with clear descriptions and enough variety for guests who would rather have chicken, seafood, vegetables, or dessert.
