THE HERKY EXPERIENCE

Reasons to Gather

A restaurant's calendar can give guests a reason to return beyond their usual favorite meal. Our fictional events program imagines weekday specials, seasonal menus, family celebrations, and opportunities to connect with the Iowa City community.

We want the calendar to feel useful rather than cluttered. Each promotion should explain the occasion, what guests can expect, and whether it applies to lunch, dinner, or a special event. Clear terms are important because confusing offers can damage trust.

Every event on this page is a creative example for a class project. There are no scheduled promotions, ticket sales, or actual restaurant events.

THE HERKY EXPERIENCE

Seasonal Ideas

Graduation weekends can bring visiting families together, while colder months may be a good time for comforting specials. Summer could emphasize patio dining and lighter menu options. These are marketing concepts, not claims about demand or future restaurant operations.

A real restaurant would track which promotions bring guests back, whether offers improve revenue after costs, and whether staff can deliver the experience consistently. The site could then highlight the events that customers actually value.

THE HERKY EXPERIENCE

What Herky Stands For

Herky Steakhouse is an imagined Iowa City restaurant built around a simple idea: a great dinner should feel special without feeling exclusive. Our concept blends generous portions, warm service, familiar favorites, and a comfortable room where families, friends, students, and coworkers can gather. Every offering on this site is a fictional example created for a digital marketing course.

The proposed downtown location is part of the story rather than an operating address. The building visualization shows a possible street-level restaurant with apartments above, not an existing business or an approved construction project. The hours, prices, menu descriptions, job opportunities, and promotions are illustrative and should not be treated as available services.

We designed the experience for different kinds of evenings: a quick lunch between commitments, a family meal after a long week, a birthday celebration, or a relaxed date night. The menu aims for approachable choices alongside premium cuts, with clear descriptions and enough variety for guests who would rather have chicken, seafood, vegetables, or dessert.
