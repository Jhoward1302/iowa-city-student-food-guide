Our Fictional Story

Good Food. Good Company.

Built Around Getting Together

Herky Steakhouse is an imagined Iowa City restaurant created for a university digital marketing project. Its fictional founders wanted to pair the cozy welcome of a neighborhood grill with the polish of a special-occasion steakhouse.

The concept is simple: a date-night setting that still feels comfortable for family dinners, birthdays, and celebrations. Warm wood interiors, an approachable menu, and thoughtful hospitality shape the vision.
