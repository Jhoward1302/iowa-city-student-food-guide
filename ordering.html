THE HERKY EXPERIENCE

Ordering Made Simple

For a busy family or a guest on a short break, online ordering should make dinner planning easier. A useful ordering page shows menu choices, prices, quantities, and a running subtotal without forcing visitors through confusing steps. Our demo uses a small selection of fictional dishes to illustrate that experience.

Guests can add items to a local cart, adjust quantities, and review an example order. The cart stays in this browser session. There is no kitchen, payment processor, pickup counter, or order management system behind it, so nothing is purchased or prepared.

A real ordering operation would need secure payments, accurate inventory, food preparation estimates, clear taxes and fees, and policies for allergies and substitutions. Those services are intentionally not implemented in this student project.

THE HERKY EXPERIENCE

Designed for Everyday Convenience

We imagine Herky Steakhouse as a place where guests can enjoy a comfortable dining room or bring a familiar favorite home. The menu emphasizes recognizable meals and clear pricing because that helps visitors compare options quickly.

Online ordering is also a digital marketing tool: it reduces the steps between discovering a dish and deciding to buy it. In this demonstration, we can show the interface without collecting real customer data or claiming that a transaction occurred.

THE HERKY EXPERIENCE

What Herky Stands For

Herky Steakhouse is an imagined Iowa City restaurant built around a simple idea: a great dinner should feel special without feeling exclusive. Our concept blends generous portions, warm service, familiar favorites, and a comfortable room where families, friends, students, and coworkers can gather. Every offering on this site is a fictional example created for a digital marketing course.

The proposed downtown location is part of the story rather than an operating address. The building visualization shows a possible street-level restaurant with apartments above, not an existing business or an approved construction project. The hours, prices, menu descriptions, job opportunities, and promotions are illustrative and should not be treated as available services.

We designed the experience for different kinds of evenings: a quick lunch between commitments, a family meal after a long week, a birthday celebration, or a relaxed date night. The menu aims for approachable choices alongside premium cuts, with clear descriptions and enough variety for guests who would rather have chicken, seafood, vegetables, or dessert.
