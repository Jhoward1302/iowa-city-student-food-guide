THE HERKY EXPERIENCE

Food for the Whole Table

Our dinner menu is built to balance familiar steakhouse favorites with a little extra polish. A guest looking for a straightforward sirloin can find one, while someone celebrating a milestone can choose a larger cut. The idea is to make a special meal accessible without asking every guest to spend the same amount.

Not everyone wants steak. Chicken, seafood, ribs, salads, and comforting sides give mixed groups a reason to dine together. We also imagine weekday lunch combinations and family-friendly choices that keep a casual visit practical.

Prices are fictional planning examples, not current offers or comparisons to an operating restaurant. A real menu would need accurate ingredient sourcing, portion standards, nutritional information, allergen procedures, and regular price reviews before publication.

Use the menu controls to inspect the printed-style layout. You can also explore our separate lunch, drinks and desserts, and kids menu pages. Those sections demonstrate how a restaurant can organize a larger catalog without making its main dinner menu difficult to navigate.
